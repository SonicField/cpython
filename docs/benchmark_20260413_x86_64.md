## Auto vs Force Analysis
- Force improves warmup-sensitive benchmarks: try_except 0.58→1.37x, positional_dispatch 0.83→1.78x, context_manager 0.99→1.50x
- Force worsens others: kwargs 1.00→0.71x, richards_full 1.00→0.78x, store_subscr 1.00→0.93x
- Net: auto 0.92x → force 0.98x (0.06x improvement from eliminating warmup)
- Even at best (force), JIT is break-even, not the prior 1.10x
- pytorch_cm CRASHES with force_compile — new bug

## Two Distinct Issues
1. **Warmup overhead (auto-specific):** Functions not reaching threshold=1000, running interpreted
2. **Codegen overhead (fundamental):** JIT generates slower code than interpreter for kwargs, generators, decorators, nn_module patterns
